Summary
ID: Email Draf riset siap direview kini menyisipkan link Buka halaman riset (/id/konten/riset/{id}) di atas link review. / EN: The research-draft-ready email now inserts an Open research page link (/id/konten/riset/{id}) above the review link.

Prior Condition & Problem

ID: Email hanya menautkan halaman review; penerima tak bisa lompat langsung ke halaman riset sesi terkait. / EN: The email only linked the review page; recipients could not jump straight to the related session research page.

Reason & Motivation

ID: Mempercepat navigasi admin dari notifikasi email ke konteks riset penuh. / EN: Speeds admin navigation from the email notification to full research context.

Applied Solution

ID: Input sendDraftReadyEmail tambah field opsional researchSessionId; body HTML sisipkan paragraf tautan riset sebelum paragraf review; runner meneruskan sessionId yang sudah ada di scope. / EN: Added an optional researchSessionId field to the sendDraftReadyEmail input; the HTML body inserts the research link paragraph before the review paragraph; the runner passes the already-in-scope sessionId.

  • src/lib/automation/runner.ts
